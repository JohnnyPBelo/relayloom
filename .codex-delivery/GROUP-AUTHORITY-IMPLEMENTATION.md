## Admission trust and ordering

A newcomer explicitly trusts the creator and the signed parent roster it consents against. It validates its own exact fresh consent in the joined snapshot; this never grants historical message keys. Once locally admitted, every intermediate private membership snapshot during that uninterrupted membership must validate before current participation becomes active. Merely receiving a higher signed header or a later valid-looking snapshot cannot bypass a missing/invalid intermediate transition.

Root built a genuine counterexample with a creator-signed intermediate roster missing join consent, followed by an otherwise valid successor. The first regression failed: state incorrectly became active. `checkedThrough` is now a durable local validation cursor, initialized at creator birth or the exact fresh join, advanced only across retained verified snapshots, and cleared on leave/removal/card replacement. Later snapshots may wait until earlier valid state arrives. The regression passed after correction. Its original failure is `.cache/group-registry-evidence/intermediate-before.log`. This is root review, not independent review.

## Storage and bounds

One group checkpoint contains the permanent anchor, last adopted header, local admission/cursor, pending invitation/consent, local-left fence and concrete fork/capacity evidence. At most64 groups including left/closed/forked records. Require the4MiB reserve and cap each checkpoint at52KiB, leaving index/envelope overhead within the reserve. Public headers and private snapshots remain separate non-expiring authority records; ordinary content TTL does not erase them. Unknown hints cannot enroll a group. Pages remain16 headers/256KiB.

Up to256 operation records provide finite idempotence. Retention order uses the authenticated storage revision, not wall-clock ordering. Eviction of an operation never evicts group proofs or fences. UI must not automatically retry an uncertain operation after its record was retired.
